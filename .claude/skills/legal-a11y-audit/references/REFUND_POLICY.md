# Refund Policy

**Not applicable, do not publish a refund page as it is.** Lingkaran takes no payments. There is no subscription, no checkout and no fee, so there is nothing to refund. The app shows on every marketplace screen: "Lingkaran does not handle payments or delivery. Deal directly with the seller and check before you pay."

Publish this short text (for example inside the Terms, section 3) so nobody expects a refund from you:

> Lingkaran is free. We do not sell anything and we do not take payment. Refunds, returns and delivery are between the buyer and the seller. We cannot refund or reverse a payment you made to another person. If a seller cheated you, report the product in the app and contact the police or your payment provider.
