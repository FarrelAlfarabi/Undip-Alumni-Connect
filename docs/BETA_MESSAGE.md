# Messages for the beta

Two drafts. Fill the brackets. Plain words, no jargon.

## To Ikafe leadership (or whoever was promised the features)

Subject: Lingkaran beta, what is ready and what comes next

All the promised features are in the beta: the alumni directory, jobs, business
directory, marketplace, request to contact, notifications, and the admin tools.

One thing is not finished yet: a real sign in. Today the app checks your email
against the Ikafe list, but it does not prove you own that email. Because of this,
a technical person with the app file could read or change more than they should.

So for the first round we will do three things:
1. Only [number] named testers get the app. It is not public.
2. Testers use test contact details, not real CVs or real phone numbers.
3. The real alumni list is not loaded yet.

The real sign in (a code sent to your email) is planned next. It needs about
[3 to 4 weeks]. After it ships, we can load the real list and open the beta wider.

A short delay now protects the members. One leak of CVs or phone numbers would
cost far more trust than the delay.

What I need from you: [the contact email and postal address to show in the privacy policy], and a date by which the first testers should have the app.

## To testers (goes with the APK)

Hi [name],

Thank you for testing Lingkaran. This is a closed test, so please:
- Use test contact details. Do not upload a real CV. Do not enter a real phone number.

- Do not forward the app file or share the link.

work, send a screenshot to [contact email].

The app will ask you to update once during the test. We will tell you before.

You can ask us to delete your account at any time by writing to [contact email].

Thank you,
