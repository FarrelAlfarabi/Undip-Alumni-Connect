# UX review of Lingkaran (closed beta)

This is a code reading review. I read the screens, widgets, lock and policy files and imagined a first time user on a 360dp wide phone, comparing with Tokopedia (marketplace), LinkedIn (directory, jobs) and WhatsApp (contact, notifications). Nothing was run or tested on a real phone, so some layout points (marked "may") need a quick check on a device. The chat screens are switched off for the beta, so they were only skimmed. Rows are ordered with the most useful first. Existing Indonesian copy is kept as it is.

| Screen | Problem | Severity | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consent, Policy (policy_config.dart:13-14, policy_text.dart:20-27) | Operator and contact email are still the placeholders `[FILL IN: operator name]` and `[FILL IN: contact email]`. A first time user sees them on the policy at the consent step, and the policy tells people to email that address to delete their data. | High | Put the real operator name and email in `kOperatorName` and `kContactEmail`. Add a test that fails if the filled text contains `[FILL IN`. | Open. I must fill in the two values in `lib/config/policy_config.dart` |
| Marketplace list, detail, form, My listings (marketplace_demo_notice.dart:7, used at marketplace_screen.dart:296, marketplace_detail_screen.dart:135) | The banner "Demo only, no real payments" sits next to real businesses, real shop links and real contact info. It tells buyers nothing is real, and it hides the true risk: payment and delivery happen outside the app, directly with the seller. Tokopedia has buyer protection, here there is none. | High | Change the text to: "Lingkaran does not handle payments or delivery. Deal directly with the seller and check before you pay." Show it also on the Businesses segment. Update tests that read `MarketplaceDemoNotice.text`. | Fixed (Stage 8) |
| Verification (verification_screen.dart:293-296, 373-376) and Welcome (welcome_screen.dart:128-131) | Copy says "demo check against seeded sample data", "try one of the sample demo accounts" and "Demo build with dummy data". No sample accounts are listed anywhere, so a tester whose email is not found has no next step. The copy also says data is dummy while real emails and phone numbers are used. | High | Replace with: "Use the email the Ikafe team has for you. If it is not found, contact the beta team." and add a real contact (same value as `kContactEmail`). Drop "dummy data" wording or say the beta uses real member data. | Fixed (Stage 8) |
| Verification (verification_screen.dart:16-33, 86-105) | Anyone who knows an alumnus email becomes that person: no code, no link, no password. They can then request contacts, post as them, or delete their account (delete_account_screen.dart:30-31 admits this). | High | Real fix is an email code or magic link, not a small UI change. Until then, state it in the beta notes and keep Delete account behind a second check. | Open. Needs real login (email code); not small |
| Profile menu, About (about_screen.dart:29-33, profile_detail_screen.dart:457-510) | About says: use "Send feedback" when something looks wrong. But that button only appears after an error. Dead end. Medium effort (city field); not done in a free-launch review |
| Requests, Received (requests_screen.dart:230, 249-271) | The card shows only the name and an optional message. LinkedIn shows the profile before you accept. | High | Make the card open the requester profile (read only) and show major and class year. Needs the requester id, which `IncomingRequest` already has. | Open. Medium effort; not done in Stage 8 |
| Requests, Received (requests_screen.dart:259-263) | Decline is one tap with no confirmation and no undo, right next to Accept. The requester only sees "Not accepted". | Medium | Ask "Decline this request?" with Cancel and Decline, or show an Undo snackbar. | Open |
| Marketplace, Add a product (marketplace_screen.dart:216-228, marketplace_messages.dart:33-35) | A user whose business is still "Waiting for review" taps Add a product and is told to register a business first, with a "Register a business" button. This invites duplicate registrations. | Medium | If `mine` is not empty but none is approved, say "Your business is waiting for review" and offer "My businesses" instead. | Open |
| Marketplace detail (marketplace_detail_screen.dart:209-233) | "Contact seller" only reveals plain text. No tap to open WhatsApp or the phone, no copy button. A seller number has to be selected by hand. | Medium | Add a Copy button, and an "Open WhatsApp" button when the text looks like a phone number (wa.me link). | Open |
| Marketplace detail, Business detail (marketplace_detail_screen.dart:238-241, business_directory_screen.dart:347-364) | Business detail has no products, no owner profile link and no contact path. Only two outside links. Listing detail has the seller card, so the two screens feel inconsistent. | Medium | On Business detail add the owner card (tap opens profile, then Request to contact) and a list of that business products. | Open |
| Home (home_screen.dart:233-240, 252) | The "Own a business?" card (about 140dp) sits above the quick tiles for every non owner, after a 172dp banner. On a 360x640 phone the tiles start below the fold, and the card keeps showing every visit. | Medium | Move the card below the tiles, or show it as a small dismissible row. | Open |
| Home tiles (home_screen.dart:388-392, 467-473) | At 360dp each tile text gets about 89dp. "Marketplace" and "Nearby Alumni" may be cut or wrap. 5 tiles in 2 columns leaves one tile alone. | Medium | Use one line labels at labelMedium, or a 3 column grid, and check on a 360dp device. | Open |
| Home app bar (home_screen.dart:144-203) | Notifications and Requests are icon only. The handshake icon does not say "Requests". The bell and handshake exist only on Home, so from Directory, Market or Profile you never see a badge. WhatsApp puts counts on tabs. | Medium | Show a count badge on the Home tab icon, and add a short "Requests" shortcut on the Directory profile screens. | Open |
| Sent request snackbar (request_contact_sheet.dart:27-28, 106-111) | After Send request the only message is "Request sent to X." It does not say where the answer will appear, and there is no Requests link. | Medium | Say "Request sent to X. You will see the answer in Requests (handshake icon on Home)." with a "View" snackbar action. | Open |
| Profile, Sign out (profile_detail_screen.dart:187-197) | Sign out is an icon only button in the top corner (where users look for settings) and signs out at once. It also forgets the remembered person on this device. No confirmation. | Medium | Show a confirm dialog "Sign out of this phone?" before `signOutTo`. | Open |
| Lock screen (lock_screen.dart:233-246, 289-295) | "Forgot PIN? Verify again" and "Not you? Switch account" wipe local data with no confirmation. The 5th wrong PIN also wipes, but the screen never warns about this, it only shows "N attempts left" after the first miss. | Medium | Add a confirm dialog on both links, and say "5 wrong PINs clears this phone" on the pad. | Open |
| Profile view of other alumni (profile_detail_screen.dart:274) | The NIM (student number) of every alumnus is shown to every other member. Email is hidden, NIM is not. | Medium | Show NIM only when `showEditButton` is true (own profile), or confirm with Ikafe that it is meant to be public. | Open |
| Apply to job, done step (apply_job_screen.dart:444-448, job_detail_screen.dart:252-257) | Done text says "You can check its status any time from this job", but job detail only shows a disabled "Applied" button. | Applicants (job_applicants_screen.dart:40-43) | CV links are built with `getPublicUrl`, and the apply screen never tells the applicant that anyone with the link can open the CV. | Medium | Verify the `cvs` bucket is private and use a signed link. Add one line of copy under the CV button. | Open |
| Delete account, My businesses, Marketplace limit (delete_account_screen.dart:185-190, my_businesses_screen.dart:200-205, marketplace_messages.dart:38-41) | Three screens say "contact an admin" or "the Ikafe team can restore you", but none shows who or how. | Feedback sheet (feedback_sheet.dart:11-12) | The failure message says "send them on WhatsApp" but gives no number or link. | Medium | Add a "Send on WhatsApp" button with the beta number, or change the text to "Send to <email>". | Open |
| Business form (business_form_screen.dart:273-284) | Asks for yearly sales with no reason given. "Large (not UMKM)" does not say what happens. Only admins see it." and say what happens for Large. | Open |
| Marketplace form (marketplace_form_screen.dart:216-317, 464) | Required marks are missing (the business form uses `*`). The photo is required, but the hint only says "One photo. | Directory (directory_screen.dart:153-181) | Three dropdowns in one row at 360dp leave about 104dp each, so values like a long major name are cut to "Manaj...". The row is also about 40dp tall (filter_dropdown.dart:31-33), under 48dp. | Medium | Use two rows, or open a filter bottom sheet. Raise the vertical padding to 12. | Open |
| Directory (directory_screen.dart:222) | Subtitle prints the text "null" when a major or graduation year is missing (`${p['major']} · Class of ${p['graduation_year']}`). | Medium | Build the line from the non empty parts only. | Open |
| Consent (consent_screen.dart:87-148) | The bottom area (4 line bilingual checkbox, Continue, Back) takes about 220dp, and the reader above also has a banner and language switch. | Market tab names (home_screen.dart:402, home_shell.dart:151, market_screen.dart:51, marketplace_detail_screen.dart:122) | Same area has five names: "Marketplace" tile, "Market" tab, "Products" segment, "Listing" detail, "Add a product".
